# How to use the trade packs

The Unghosted System works for any trade. These packs make it *specific*: the right reasons, the right seasons and the right objections for your work. Each pack contains:

1. **Missed-call text**, tuned to the trade
2. **Quote angle**: the one sentence that answers the trade's most common hesitation
3. **12-month campaign calendar**: what to send, and when
4. **Two seasonal campaigns**, ready to send
5. **Maintenance plan example**, with numbers to adjust
6. **Top 3 objections**, with honest replies

Fill-ins are the same as the main system: `{Business}`, `{You}`, `{First}`, `{Link}`, `{ReviewLink}`, `{Offer}`.

> **Seasons:** calendars assume a typical U.S. four-season climate. In the Sun Belt, shift cooling campaigns about a month earlier and shorten heating ones. In the Southern Hemisphere, flip them.

# HVAC

**Missed-call text**
> Hi, {You} with {Business}. Sorry I missed you, I'm on a call. Is your system not heating/cooling right now? Reply with what's happening and your zip and I'll get back to you fast. No heat or AC with kids or seniors at home? Reply URGENT.

**Quote angle (replacement quotes):**
> "The price covers the equipment, the install to code, permits, haul-away of the old unit, and the {X}-year labor warranty, so you won't get surprise bills later."

**12-month calendar**

| Month | Campaign |
|---|---|
| Jan | Heating check for anyone who skipped fall; maintenance plan push |
| Feb | Indoor air quality (filters, humidifiers) |
| Mar | **Spring AC tune-up campaign** (launch) |
| Apr | AC tune-up reminder + plan offer |
| May | Last call before summer; old-system replacement quotes follow-up |
| Jun | Referral push (busy season, happy customers) |
| Jul | Emergency-ready text: "here's what to check before you call" |
| Aug | Replacement financing campaign for older systems |
| Sep | **Fall furnace tune-up campaign** (launch) |
| Oct | Furnace tune-up reminder + carbon monoxide safety |
| Nov | Plan renewal / annual plan offer |
| Dec | Thank-you + "before the holidays" heating check |

**Seasonal campaign 1: Spring AC tune-up**
- *Text:* "Hi {First}, {You} from {Business}. AC season is around the corner, and a spring tune-up is the cheapest way to avoid a July breakdown. I have openings {weeks}. Want one?"
- *Email subject:* "Before the first hot day" · *Body:* 3 sentences: most AC failures happen on the first heat wave; a tune-up checks refrigerant, electrical and drainage; reply with a day or book at {Link}.

**Seasonal campaign 2: Fall furnace check**
- *Text:* "Hi {First}, it's {You} at {Business}. Before you turn the heat on for the season, we can do a safety check (burners, heat exchanger, CO). Openings {weeks}. Want me to save you a spot?"
- *Email subject:* "Turn the heat on with confidence"

**Maintenance plan example:** *Comfort Club*: $17/month · spring AC + fall heating tune-up · priority service · 15% off repairs · no overtime fees.

**Top objections**
1. *"It's still working, why tune it up?"* → "Totally fair. Most tune-ups are about catching a $20 part before it takes out a $1,500 one. If it checks out fine, you've got peace of mind for summer."
2. *"Another company quoted less."* → "Could you check whether theirs includes the permit, haul-away and labor warranty? If it does and it's apples to apples, I'll tell you honestly whether I can match it."
3. *"I need to think about the replacement."* → "Of course. It's a big decision. Want me to send a repair-vs-replace comparison so you can see both numbers side by side?"
