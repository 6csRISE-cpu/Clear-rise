# Partner & Affiliate Terms

*Last updated: November 22, 2026*

These terms apply to anyone who recommends Unghosted for a commission ("partners" or "affiliates"), including consultants, bookkeepers, agencies and virtual assistants. By joining the program you agree to them.

## 1. Commission
- **30% of the net sale price** (after refunds, taxes and discounts) of any Unghosted product bought through your tracked link, including the first payment of a Ghost Club membership.
- Tracking is handled by our store platform (Payhip) using your unique link and a cookie that lasts **60 days**.
- Commissions on orders refunded within the 30-day guarantee period are reversed.
- Payouts are made **monthly by PayPal** for the prior month's confirmed commissions once your balance reaches **$25**.
- You may not earn commission on your own purchases, or on purchases by your own business.

## 2. You must disclose that you earn a commission
U.S. law (the FTC Act and the FTC's Endorsement Guides) requires anyone paid to recommend a product to **clearly disclose that relationship.** Every time you recommend Unghosted with your link, whether in an email, post, video, newsletter, website, text or conversation with a client, you must:

- Say plainly that you earn a commission, **close to the recommendation** (before or right next to the link, not buried in a footer or behind a "more" button).
- Use plain words. Good: *"I earn a commission if you buy through my link."* / *"Paid link: I get a commission at no extra cost to you."* Not enough on its own: "affiliate link," "#aff," "#sp," or a disclosure only on a separate page.
- In videos, say it out loud **and** show it on screen. In short social posts, put it at the start of the post.
- If you're an advisor (bookkeeper, consultant, agency, VA) recommending Unghosted to your **own clients**, tell them about the commission before they buy.

## 3. What you may not do
- **No spam.** No unsolicited bulk email, and **no texts to anyone who hasn't given you written consent** to marketing texts. (We sell texting-compliance guidance, so we hold partners to it.)
- **No false or exaggerated claims.** Don't promise specific income or results, don't say Unghosted guarantees legal compliance, and don't invent features.
- **No fake reviews or testimonials,** and no reviews of Unghosted that hide your commission.
- **No paid search on our brand.** Don't bid on "Unghosted" or misspellings in Google/Bing ads, and don't run ads that look like they come from us.
- **No coupon or deal sites** unless we approve it in writing.
- **No impersonation.** Don't present yourself as Unghosted staff, and don't register domains or social handles using our name.
- **No sharing or reselling our files.** Recommend the product. Don't distribute it.

## 4. Partner License (agencies, consultants and VAs)
If you implement Unghosted *for* client businesses, buy the **Partner License** instead of sending each client to buy separately. It covers use of the templates with up to **10 client businesses** you actively serve. You may adapt the templates and load them into your clients' tools under your own brand. You may not resell or give away the Unghosted files or substantial parts of them as a product. Need more than 10 clients? Contact us.

## 5. Ending the program
You can leave any time. We may remove anyone who breaks these terms, and unpaid commissions on orders obtained in breach are forfeited. We may change these terms with 30 days' notice by email. Changes don't affect commissions already earned.

## 6. Independent relationship
You're an independent partner, not an employee or agent of Unghosted. You're responsible for your own taxes on commissions.
