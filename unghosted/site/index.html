Do I need special software?

No. You can send everything from your phone and a free email account. If you use a field-service app (Jobber, Housecall Pro, ServiceTitan, Workiz and similar) or a business texting app, you can load the templates once and let them send automatically.

How do I get the files?

Is texting customers legal?

It depends on the kind of text, which is why every template is labeled. Replying to someone who called you is the lowest-risk kind. Messages about a booked job or a requested quote need the customer's OK to text. Marketing texts (offers, "your tune-up is due") need their written consent first, and automated marketing needs more. The Kit includes The Texting Rules, a plain-English guide with sample consent wording, quiet hours and opt-out rules. It's practical guidance, not legal advice.

Are these AI-generated templates?

They're written and edited for how homeowners and tradespeople actually talk: short, specific and honest. We include AI prompts so you can quickly adapt them to your own voice, but the templates themselves are ready to use as-is.
