# Why this matters

Call-tracking studies of home-service businesses find that somewhere between **1 in 7 and 1 in 4 calls go unanswered**. When they do, many callers don't leave a voicemail. They call the next company on the list.

You can't answer every call when you're under a sink or on a roof. But you *can* reply within 5 minutes with a text. That one habit wins back jobs you'd otherwise never know you lost.

Here are the five texts. Replace `{Business}`, `{You}` and `{First}` with your details.

---

# Text 1 — The instant reply (business hours)
> Hi, this is {You} with {Business}. Sorry I missed your call, I'm on a job right now. What can I help you with? Reply here and I'll get back to you within the hour.

**Why it works:** it's personal, it explains *why* you missed them, and it asks a question, so it's easy to reply to.

# Text 2 — After hours
> Hi, it's {You} at {Business}. We're closed for the night, but I saw your call. Reply with what you need and the best time to reach you, and I'll call first thing in the morning. If it's an emergency, reply URGENT.

# Text 3 — The second touch (2–3 hours later, no reply)
> Just checking you got sorted, {First}. Still happy to help with that if you need it. I have an opening {tomorrow morning / Thursday afternoon}.

**Why it works:** a specific opening turns "maybe" into "yes, Thursday works."

# Text 4 — The final touch (next day)
> No worries if you found someone! If you still need help, we're here. Just reply to this text. — {You}, {Business}

Three touches, then stop.

# Text 5 — The quote nudge (3 days after sending a quote)
> Hi {First}, it's {You} from {Business}. Just making sure the quote came through OK. Any questions at all, reply here. Happy to explain anything or put together a smaller option.

---

# Set it up in 5 minutes
1. Open your business phone app or field-service software and search its settings for **"missed call text"** or **"auto-reply."**
2. Paste Text 1. Save.
3. Call your own number from another phone and let it ring out. Did the text arrive? 

*Replying to someone who just called you is conversational. Promotional texts need the customer's written consent. Always honor STOP.*
