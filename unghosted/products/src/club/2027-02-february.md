# This month's focus: dead quotes and early spring bookings

Every business has a pile of quotes from the last 6–12 months that never closed. Some of those people still need the work and just never got around to it. February is the perfect time to revive them: budgets reset in January, tax refunds arrive in February and March, and spring projects are on people's minds.

**Your February checklist (about 1 hour)**
- ☐ Week 1: pull every unclosed quote from the last 12 months
- ☐ Week 1: send the "Quote Revival" message
- ☐ Week 2–3: send the "Spring Pre-Book" campaign to past customers
- ☐ Week 4: GBP post, fix of the month, scorecard

---

# Campaign 1: Quote Revival

**Who gets it:** anyone who received a quote from you in the last 12 months and didn't book. These people contacted you about their own project, but it's been a while, so this is **Type 3 · Marketing**: email them, and text only those who gave written marketing consent.

**Email**
**Subject:** Is your {Job} project still on the list?

> Hi {First},
>
> Last {month} I put together a quote for your {Job}. I'm reaching out because a lot of people put projects like this on hold and then want to get them done before spring.
>
> If it's still on your list, I'm happy to {refresh the quote / come take another look / put together a smaller first phase}. Prices on some materials have changed, so I'll make sure you have an accurate number.
>
> And if you've already had it done, or decided against it, just let me know and I'll close it out.
>
> {You}, {Business}

**Text (written marketing consent only)**
> Hi {First}, {You} from {Business}. I quoted your {Job} last {month}. Is it still on your list? Happy to refresh the quote before spring if you'd like.

**Tip:** sort these by quote size and *call* the top five. Big quotes deserve a real conversation.

# Campaign 2: Spring Pre-Book

**Message type → 3 · Marketing** (email everyone; text only with written marketing consent).

**Email (Week 2)**
**Subject:** Reserve your spring {service} before the rush

> Hi {First},
>
> Every year, our spring schedule fills up within a few weeks of the first warm day. If you'd like your {spring service} done in {March/April}, now's the time to reserve a spot.
>
> Reply with a preferred week, or book here: {Link}. You can change or cancel with no fee.
>
> {You}

# Fix of the month: speed-to-reply test

Ask a friend to text and call your business number at three random times this month: weekday morning, weekday evening and weekend. Time how long it takes them to get a reply. Under 5 minutes is great; under 1 hour is OK; longer means jobs are leaking. Adjust your auto-text or phone forwarding until every test gets a fast reply.

# Scorecard focus: quote close rate

Count how many revived quotes turned into booked jobs. Even 1 in 10 is usually worth the hour this campaign takes.
