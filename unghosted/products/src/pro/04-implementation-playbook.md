# Part 1 — The 90-Day Plan

The 30-Day Rollout in the main system turns everything on. This plan makes it stick and grows it.

| Days | Focus | Done when… |
|---|---|---|
| 1–7 | Missed-call text-back, voicemail greeting, quote follow-up reminders | Every missed call gets a text within 5 minutes |
| 8–14 | Review Engine | Every finished job gets an in-person ask + V1 text |
| 15–21 | First rebooking batch | First monthly list exported and sent |
| 22–30 | Cancellation recovery + appointment reminders + referral cards | Reminders go out automatically |
| 31–45 | Maintenance plan launched (if it fits your trade) | Plan priced, pitched at every job, emailed to past customers |
| 46–60 | Trade Pack calendar loaded for the next 3 months | Campaigns scheduled or on your calendar |
| 61–75 | Staff trained on phone + review scripts *(Part 2)* | Everyone answering the phone uses the call flow |
| 76–90 | Review your scorecard; double down on the best campaign | Scorecard filled in for 3 months |

**Weekly habit (15 minutes, Monday morning):**
1. Check last week's quotes and send the next follow-up touches.
2. Reply to new Google reviews.
3. Glance at the scorecard numbers.

# Part 2 — Staff Scripts & Training

If anyone besides you answers the phone or finishes jobs, they're your revenue-recovery team. Run a 20-minute meeting with these.

### The 5-step call flow (print and put by every phone)
1. **Greet:** "Thanks for calling {Business}, this is {Name}. How can I help?"
2. **Listen and repeat back:** "So your AC is running but not cooling. Got it."
3. **Qualify:** address/zip · how urgent · any details (age of system, size of home)
4. **Book, don't quote over the phone:** "The best way to give you an accurate price is to have {tech} take a look. I have {day} morning or {day} afternoon. Which is better?"
5. **Confirm:** "You're booked for {day} between {window}. You'll get a text confirmation. Anything else?"

### Price-shopper script
> "I totally understand wanting a ballpark. Most {job}s for us land between ${low} and ${high}, depending on {factor}. The visit to give you an exact price is {free / $x, credited toward the job}. Want me to set that up?"

### Tech end-of-job script (30 seconds)
1. "Here's what I did and what I found…" (show, don't just tell)
2. "Everything look good to you?"
3. **Review ask (every customer, same words):** "Would you mind leaving us an honest Google review? I'll text you the link."
4. **Plan pitch (if applicable):** "Also, most of our regulars are on our {Plan}. Want me to send you the details?"
5. **Referral card:** leave it on the counter.

### Training checklist

| ☐ | Skill | Practice |
|---|---|---|
| ☐ | Answers within 3 rings using the greeting | Role-play 3 calls |
| ☐ | Books instead of quoting over the phone | Role-play price shopper |
| ☐ | Knows the missed-call text goes out automatically | Show them the setting |
| ☐ | Asks for the review in person at every job | Watch them do it once |
| ☐ | Knows the plan in one sentence | Say it out loud |
| ☐ | Knows never to offer anything in exchange for a review, never to use their own device for a customer's review, and never to suggest wording | Explain why (Google/FTC rules) |
| ☐ | Knows the 4 message types and quiet hours (8 a.m.–8 p.m.) | Walk through *The Texting Rules* quick-reference card |

# Part 3 — Software Setup Cheat Sheet

Every app's menus change over time, so instead of click-paths that go stale, here's **what to look for** and **what to search** in your app's help center.

| What you want | Search your app's help for | Where it usually lives |
|---|---|---|
| Automatic missed-call text | "missed call text back" / "auto-reply" | Phone/Communications settings |
| Saved message templates | "message templates" / "canned responses" / "snippets" | Settings → Messaging/Templates |
| Quote follow-ups | "quote follow-up" / "estimate follow-up automation" | Quotes/Estimates settings |
| Review requests after jobs | "review request" / "job follow-up" | Marketing or Automations |
| Appointment reminders | "appointment reminders" / "visit reminders" | Scheduling or Notifications |
| Customer export | "export clients" / "export customers CSV" | Clients/Customers list |
| Recurring billing (plans) | "recurring invoices" / "service plans" / "memberships" | Invoicing/Billing |

**No field-service software?** Here's the minimum setup:
- **Business phone app** with missed-call auto-text and saved replies (Google Voice for basics, or a business texting app such as Quo/OpenPhone or a similar provider)
- **Free email tool** for the rebooking and plan emails (MailerLite, Brevo and Mailchimp all have free tiers)
- **A calendar** with reminders for quote follow-up touches
- **The Unghosted tracker spreadsheet**

**Importing templates in bulk:** `Templates-Import.csv` (included) lists every template with a name, channel and text. Many apps can import a CSV of saved replies. If yours can't, copy-paste them. It's about 20 minutes, once.

# Part 4 — Google Business Profile Checklist

Your Google Business Profile is the single biggest free source of calls for a local trade. Run through this once, then keep it fresh monthly.

**One-time setup**
- ☐ Business name exactly as on your signage/paperwork (no keyword stuffing; Google can suspend for it)
- ☐ Primary category is your core trade (e.g. "HVAC contractor", "House cleaning service"); add 2–4 relevant secondary categories
- ☐ Service area set to the towns/zips you actually serve
- ☐ Hours accurate, including holiday hours
- ☐ Phone number is the one with missed-call text-back turned on
- ☐ Website/booking link added
- ☐ Services list filled in with short descriptions and price ranges where possible
- ☐ 10+ real photos: team, truck, before/after, finished work (no stock photos)
- ☐ Business description (750 characters): who you serve, what you do, what makes you different
- ☐ Q&A: add and answer 3–5 common questions yourself

**Monthly (10 minutes)**
- ☐ Post one update: a recent job, a seasonal tip or the month's campaign
- ☐ Add 3–5 new photos
- ☐ Reply to every new review
- ☐ Check that hours and holiday hours are right

# Part 5 — Offer & Pricing Builder

Use this before any campaign that includes an offer. A good offer is genuine, simple and profitable.

| Question | Your answer |
|---|---|
| What's the offer? (e.g. "$30 off a tune-up," "free filter with service") | |
| What does it cost me? (parts + time) | |
| Typical job value when someone takes it | |
| Does the average job still make a profit after the offer? | Yes / No |
| Is there a real end date or limit? (only say so if true) | |
| Who gets it? (past customers / new / members only) | |

**Rules for honest offers**
- Offers are marketing (**Type 3/4**): text them only to customers with written marketing consent; email everyone else.
- Only use "limited," "ends Friday" or "only 5 spots" if it's actually true.
- Show the real price. No hidden fees revealed later.
- Discounts for referrals and loyalty are great. Discounts in exchange for reviews are not allowed.

# Part 6 — The Owner's Monthly Hour

Put this on your calendar for the 1st of every month:

1. **10 min:** fill in the Revenue Recovery Scorecard
2. **10 min:** export this month's rebooking list and send batch R1
3. **10 min:** check the Trade Pack calendar and load this month's campaign
4. **10 min:** update your Google Business Profile (post + photos)
5. **10 min:** look at the weakest scorecard number and pick one fix (use AI Prompt #10)
6. **10 min:** thank your team; share the month's best review

That's the whole system. One hour a month, plus a few seconds per job.
