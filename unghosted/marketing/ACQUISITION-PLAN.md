# Unghosted Customer Acquisition Plan (v3: partner-led, all home-service trades)

**Goal:** the first 25 paying customers within ~60–90 days, through people who already have home-service owners' trust, while slower channels build in the background.

## Decisions behind this plan
- **Many trades, one promise.** We sell to home-service businesses broadly (HVAC, plumbing, electrical, cleaning, lawn care, pest control, roofing and more) under one promise: **"Stop losing jobs because you didn't follow up."** Trade playbooks make it feel specific to each buyer. *(v2 narrowed to HVAC & plumbing only; the owner reversed that.)*
- **Partners lead, SEO follows.** SEO can take 6–12 months. Partners (consultants, contractor bookkeepers, agencies, VAs) already reach our buyers. SEO continues in the background at 1 guide/month.
- **The System is the product we optimize.** The $27 Kit is the easy first purchase, the $67 System is the primary conversion goal, and Pro targets established businesses with staff. Ghost Club is offered *after* purchase, to customers who show interest in ongoing campaigns.
- **Measure, don't assume.** Every channel reports into the dashboard. Fix the weakest step before adding a channel.

## 90-day validation plan (what success looks like)

| Window | Focus | Success signal (planning targets, not promises) | If it misses |
|---|---|---|---|
| Days 1–30 | Launch gates done; first 45 partner contacts; free guide live | Gates green; ≥8 partner replies; ≥50 free-guide signups; first 3–5 sales | Rewrite the outreach angle; test a different partner type |
| Days 31–60 | Onboard partners; first Partner Drop sent; community answers weekly | ≥3 active partners; 10–15 cumulative sales; refund rate <10% | Interview 5 non-buyers on the free-guide list; fix the biggest objection on the sales page |
| Days 61–90 | Double down on the best-performing partner type and trade | 25+ cumulative sales; ≥30% of Kit buyers upgrade or show interest; first Ghost Club members from buyers | Decide with data: narrow to the trades that buy, adjust pricing, or add the done-for-you setup offer |

**Not a goal yet:** the $100K projection. The first 90 days exist to learn which partners, trades and messages actually convert.

## Conversion optimization plan (only once there's traffic)
1. **Measure the funnel weekly:** visitors → free-guide signups → Kit/System purchases → upgrades (dashboard).
2. **One change at a time,** in this order: headline and hero offer → System vs. Kit emphasis → pricing-section copy → checkout friction → email subject lines.
3. **Customer words beat our words:** use the feedback survey and support questions (real, with permission) to rewrite headlines. Never fabricate testimonials.
4. **Later option (not built):** a done-for-you setup service ($149–$249) that customizes templates with the customer's company name, services, hours and sequences. Add it only if buyers ask for help implementing.
