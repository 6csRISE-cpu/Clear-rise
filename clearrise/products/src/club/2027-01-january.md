# This month's focus: turn the slow season into recurring revenue

January is quiet for most trades. That makes it the best month to launch or grow a **maintenance plan**, because members give you booked work in the slow months for the rest of the year. If your trade doesn't suit a plan, use the "Quick Wins" campaign instead.

**Your January checklist (about 1 hour)**
- ☐ Week 1: pick either campaign A (plans) or B (quick wins)
- ☐ Week 1–2: send it
- ☐ Week 3: follow up with non-responders
- ☐ Week 4: GBP post, fix of the month, scorecard

---

# Campaign A: "Join the Plan" (HVAC, plumbing, pest, lawn, pool, cleaning)

Before sending, make sure your plan is set up using the Plan Builder (Revenue Recovery System, Campaign 6).

**Email 1 (Week 1)**
**Subject:** A simpler way to take care of your {home/system} this year

> Hi {First},
>
> A lot of our customers told us they'd rather not have to remember when maintenance is due. So this year we're making it simple with the {Plan name}:
>
> - {visits} per year, scheduled for you
> - Priority service when something goes wrong
> - {discount} off repairs
> - ${price}/month, cancel any time
>
> Join in January and we'll {genuine bonus: "do your first visit this month" / "waive the first month"}.
>
> Reply "PLAN" and I'll set it up.
>
> {You}, {Business}

**Text (Week 2, consented customers only)**
> Hi {First}, {You} from {Business}. Did you see our new maintenance plan? {visits} a year, priority service and {discount} off repairs for ${price}/mo. Reply PLAN and I'll send details.

**Email 2 (Week 3, non-responders)**
**Subject:** Quick question about the {Plan name}

> Hi {First}, I wanted to see if you had any questions about the plan. The most common one is "what if I don't use it?" Your included visits are scheduled for you, so you'll use them. And you can cancel any time with no fee.
>
> Reply with any question, or "PLAN" to join.

# Campaign B: "Quick Wins" (any trade)

Small, low-cost jobs are easy yeses in January and often lead to bigger work.

**Email (Week 1)**
**Subject:** 3 small jobs worth doing this month

> Hi {First},
>
> January is our quietest month, which means it's the easiest time to get on our schedule. Here are three small jobs customers often put off:
>
> 1. {small job 1: e.g. "replace smoke/CO detector batteries and test them"}
> 2. {small job 2: e.g. "fix that dripping faucet"}
> 3. {small job 3: e.g. "inspect the attic for rodent entry"}
>
> If any of these are on your list, reply with the number and I'll get you scheduled, usually within a few days this month.
>
> {You}

**Text (Week 2)**
> Hi {First}, {You} at {Business}. January is when we have the most openings. Got any small jobs on your list? Reply with what you need and I'll get you in this week.
