Will this work for my trade?

The templates are written for any home-service business that quotes jobs or has repeat customers: HVAC, plumbing, electrical, cleaning, lawn and landscaping, pest control, pool, window and gutter cleaning, garage doors, handyman and more. The Pro Bundle adds packs tuned to six trades.

Do I need special software?

No. You can send everything from your phone and a free email account. If you use a field-service app (Jobber, Housecall Pro, ServiceTitan, Workiz and similar) or a business texting app, you can load the templates once and let them send automatically.

How do I get the files?

Instantly after checkout. You'll get a download link on screen and by email: PDFs, editable Word files, a spreadsheet and a CSV of every template.

Is texting customers legal?

Replying to someone who contacted you is generally fine. Promotional texts need the customer's prior written consent. The kit includes a plain-English guide and sample consent wording. It's practical guidance, not legal advice.

Are these AI-generated templates?

They're written and edited for how homeowners and tradespeople actually talk: short, specific and honest. We include AI prompts so you can quickly adapt them to your own voice, but the templates themselves are ready to use as-is.
