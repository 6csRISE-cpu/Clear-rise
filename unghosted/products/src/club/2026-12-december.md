# This month's focus: book January now

For most trades, January is the slowest month of the year. December is when you fill it. Customers are home, thinking about the new year, and sitting on holiday cash and gift money. This pack gives you one campaign to send in the **first two weeks of December** and a short "year-end thank you" for the last week.

**Your December checklist (about 45 minutes in total)**
- ☐ Week 1: send the "Book January" campaign (below) to past customers
- ☐ Week 2: send the follow-up email
- ☐ Week 3: post the Google Business Profile update
- ☐ Week 4: send the thank-you note; fill in your scorecard

---

# Campaign: "First Slots of the Year"

**Who gets it:** everyone who used you in the last 18 months. Text only to customers who gave **written marketing consent**; email everyone else. **Message type → 3 · Marketing** (**4** if your software sends it to a list).

**Text (Dec 1–7)**
> Hi {First}, {You} from {Business}. We're opening our January schedule and wanted to offer past customers first pick before it goes public. Want me to hold a spot for your {service}?

**Email 1 (Dec 1–7)**
**Subject:** First pick of our January schedule

> Hi {First},
>
> We're opening up our January calendar, and before we announce it we're giving past customers first pick.
>
> January is a great time for {trade-specific reason: "a heating check before the coldest weeks" / "a fresh-start deep clean after the holidays" / "planning spring landscaping before the rush" / "a rodent check while it's cold"}.
>
> Reply with a week that works and I'll hold it for you, or book here: {Link}.
>
> Happy holidays,
> {You}, {Business}

**Email 2 (Dec 8–14, only to people who didn't book)**
**Subject:** Still holding a few January spots

> Hi {First}, a quick follow-up. We still have a few January openings set aside for past customers. If you'd like one, just reply with "January" and I'll send you a couple of options.
>
> {You}

**Trade-specific reasons (pick yours)**

| Trade | January reason |
|---|---|
| HVAC | Heating check before the coldest weeks; plan renewal |
| Plumbing | Frozen-pipe prevention; water heater flush after holiday guests |
| Cleaning | Post-holiday deep clean; start a recurring plan for the new year |
| Lawn/landscaping | Plan spring projects early; reserve first-round cleanups |
| Electrical | Safety inspection (space heaters, holiday lights); panel check |
| Pest control | Rodent exclusion while they're seeking warmth |

# Year-end thank you (Dec 20–31)

This one isn't a sales message, and that's the point. It keeps you top of mind for spring.

**Email**
**Subject:** Thank you for {year}

> Hi {First},
>
> As the year wraps up I wanted to say a genuine thank you. We're a small local business, and every job, every review and every friend you sent our way made a real difference this year.
>
> Wishing you and yours a great new year.
>
> {You} and the {Business} team

# Google Business Profile post

> **Now booking January.** Past customers get first pick of our new-year schedule. Need {service}? Call or book online: {Link}. Thank you for a great {year}!

*Add a photo of your team or truck. Real photos beat graphics.*

# Social / Nextdoor post

> Thank you, {town}! This year we had the privilege of {number} jobs in our community. We're opening our January schedule now. If you need {trade} help in the new year, we'd love to be there. 🙏

*(Replace {number} with your real job count, or leave that line out.)*

# Fix of the month: your voicemail greeting

Call your own business line after hours. Does the greeting tell callers to expect a text? Does a text actually arrive within 5 minutes? If not, re-record using the script in the Ghost-Proof Kit (Part 1) and test again. It takes 10 minutes and protects every missed call next year.

# Scorecard focus: rebooking rate

This month, count how many past customers you contacted and how many booked. Write the number in your scorecard. Next month's pack will help you improve it.
