A Simple Referral Program for Contractors and Home Service Businesses

Word of mouth is how most small home-service businesses grow, but most leave it to chance. A simple referral program turns "I'd recommend them" into booked jobs.

When to ask

The best moment is 3–7 days after a great experience, especially right after a customer leaves a 5-star review or sends a thank-you. They're happy, and you're on their mind.

Pick one simple reward

  • $25–$50 off their next service for every referral who books
  • A free add-on (filter change, extra room, gutter flush)
  • A donation to a local school or charity for each referral

Give the new customer something too, like $25 off their first job. Two-sided rewards get used more.

Referral rewards are fine. Rewards for reviews are not. Keep the two separate.

The referral message

A referral ask promotes your business, so send it by email, by card or in person, and by text only to customers who gave written consent to marketing texts.

Thank you so much for the kind words, {First}. It made our week! If you know a neighbor, friend or family member who needs {trade}, we'd love to help them too. Anyone you send our way gets {offer}, and you get {reward} when they book. Just have them mention your name.

The referral card

Leave a card on the counter at the end of every job:

{Business}: Know someone who needs {trade}? Give them this card. They get {offer}, and you get {reward} when they book. Referred by: ________ · {phone}

Say thank you, fast

When a referral books, tell the customer right away:

{First}, your neighbor {name} just booked with us and mentioned you. Thank you! Your {reward} is on your account.

That thank-you is what turns a one-time referrer into a repeat one.

Remind everyone twice a year

Send all past customers a short email in spring and fall: a genuine thank-you, a reminder of the reward, and nothing else.

Track it

Add a "How did you hear about us?" question to your booking form or phone script, and count referral jobs every month. If the number is zero, the program isn't visible enough. Ask in person more.
