Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

This policy explains what personal information ("ClearRise," "we") collects and how we use it. The short version: we collect only what we need to deliver your products and send you useful emails, and we never sell your information.

What we collect

  • Purchase information: your name, email address, billing country and order details. Payments are handled by Payhip and its payment processors (Stripe, PayPal). We never see or store your full card number.
  • Email subscription: your email address and first name (if given) when you download a free guide or buy a product, plus whether you open or click our emails.
  • Support messages: whatever you send us when you contact support or answer a feedback survey.
  • Website analytics: privacy-friendly, aggregate visit statistics (pages viewed, referring site, country, device type) through Cloudflare Web Analytics, which doesn't use cookies to track you across sites.

How we use it

  • To deliver your purchase, provide access to subscriptions and give support
  • To send emails you signed up for: onboarding tips for your purchase, the weekly newsletter and occasional offers. Every email has an unsubscribe link.
  • To understand which pages and products are useful, and improve them
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations

Service providers we use

  • Payhip: store, checkout, file delivery, subscriptions
  • Stripe / PayPal: payment processing
  • MailerLite: email delivery
  • Tally: support and feedback forms
  • Cloudflare: website hosting and analytics

Each processes data on our behalf under its own privacy and security terms.

Your choices and rights

  • Unsubscribe from marketing emails any time via the link in any email.
  • Access, correct or delete your data: email . We'll respond within 30 days. Depending on where you live (for example California, the EU or the UK), you may have additional rights, and we'll honor them.
  • We keep purchase records as long as required for tax and accounting (typically 7 years) and delete subscriber data within 30 days of an unsubscribe-and-delete request.

Children

Our products are for businesses. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 16.
