# Support & FAQ

Most questions are answered below. If yours isn't, <a data-link="support" data-soon="email us">contact support</a> or email <a data-mail></a>. We reply within 2 business days.

## Downloads & access
**I didn't get my download email.** Check spam/promotions first. Your download link is also on the confirmation page after checkout and in your Payhip receipt. Still missing? Email us from the address you bought with and we'll resend it.

**The download link expired / I lost the files.** Email us and we'll send a fresh link. Your purchase never expires.

**Which file do I open first?** The PDF. It's the guide. The .docx files are editable copies, the .xlsx is your tracker, and the CSV is every template in one list for importing into texting apps.

**Can I open the spreadsheet in Google Sheets?** Yes: in Google Drive choose *New → File upload*, then open it with Google Sheets.

## Using the templates
**Where do I put the missed-call text?** Search your phone or field-service app's help center for "missed call text back" or "auto-reply." The *Software Setup Cheat Sheet* (Pro) lists where it usually lives.

**Can I change the wording?** Please do. They're yours to edit. The AI prompts in the System help you rewrite them in your own voice.

**Do I need consent to text customers?** Replying to someone who contacted you is generally fine. Promotional texts need prior written consent. See "Texting the Right Way" in the Ghost-Proof Kit. *(Not legal advice.)*

## Upgrades
**I bought the Kit. Can I upgrade?** Yes. Within 30 days, use the upgrade code in your purchase email to get the System for the difference.

## Ghost Club
**When do packs arrive?** On the 1st of each month, by email, with a download link.

**How do I cancel?** Use the cancel link in your Payhip receipt or account, or email us and we'll do it for you. You keep every pack you've received.

## Refunds
30-day money-back guarantee on all one-time purchases. Just email us. See the <a href="legal/refunds.html">Refund Policy</a>.

## Licensing
One purchase covers one business and its team. Agencies serving multiple clients: email us about a multi-business license.
