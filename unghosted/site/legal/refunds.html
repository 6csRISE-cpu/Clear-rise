Refund Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

30-day money-back guarantee (one-time purchases)

If a Unghosted product (Ghost-Proof Kit, Unghosted System or Pro Bundle) isn't useful to your business, email within 30 days of purchase with your order email address, and we'll refund the full purchase price to your original payment method. You don't need to give a reason, though we appreciate hearing one.

Refunds are usually processed within 3 business days. Your bank may take 5–10 business days to show the credit.

Ghost Club (subscription)

  • You can cancel any time from the link in your Payhip receipt or account. Cancellation stops future charges, and you keep access to packs you've already received.
  • If you were charged for a renewal you didn't intend to make, email us within 7 days of the charge and we'll refund that renewal.
  • Annual plans can be refunded in full within 30 days of the first purchase.

Fair use

We may decline refunds where there's clear abuse, such as repeated purchase-and-refund cycles. This has never been an issue for genuine customers.

Your consumer rights

Nothing in this policy limits any rights you have under the consumer laws of your country or state.
