# 90-Day Launch & Publishing Calendar

Who does it: **C** = Claude (scheduled task or on request) · **O** = owner · **V** = optional VA or scheduling tool

## Weeks 1–2: Launch
| Day | Action | Who |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Owner setup steps 1–4 (`ops/OWNER-SETUP.md`) | O |
| 1 | Paste Payhip links into `site/config.js` → site goes fully live | C |
| 2 | Test purchase of every product (100% coupon) → confirm delivery + emails | O/C |
| 2 | Submit sitemap to Google + Bing | O |
| 3 | Upload 12 Pinterest pins with the scheduler (2/week) | V |
| 3–14 | Send 40 affiliate/partner outreach emails (10/day max) | V/C |
| 4 | Post first 3 short videos | V |
| 7 | Answer 5 community questions (answer bank) | O/V, optional |
| 14 | First dashboard check: visitors, signups, orders | C |

## Weeks 3–12: Steady state
| Cadence | Action | Who |
|---|---|---|
| Tuesdays | Weekly newsletter (pre-written in batches of 12) | MailerLite scheduled |
| 3×/week | Short video (from the 12 scripts, then new batches) | V |
| 2×/month | New SEO guide (topics list in ACQUISITION-PLAN.md) | C |
| Monthly, 1st | New Club pack published + broadcast | C drafts, O uploads |
| Monthly | Affiliate follow-ups + 20 new outreach emails | V/C |
| Day 30, 60, 90 | Performance review against the dashboard → one improvement | C |
