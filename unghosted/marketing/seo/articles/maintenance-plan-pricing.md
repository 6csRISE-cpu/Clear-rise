A maintenance plan (also called a service agreement, membership or club) is one of the best things a small home-service business can add. It gives you **predictable monthly income**, **booked work in slow months** and **customers who don't shop around**.

Here's how to price one simply, without a consultant.

## Step 1: Decide what's included

Keep it to three or four things a homeowner instantly understands:

- **Visits:** e.g. 2 tune-ups a year (HVAC), 1 annual inspection + flush (plumbing), 4 treatments (pest)
- **Priority:** members jump the line when something breaks
- **Discount:** 10–15% off repairs
- **No trip fee / no overtime fee** (optional)

## Step 2: Price it so the visits pay for themselves

Start with what the included visits would cost at your normal price, then spread it over 12 months.

| Example | Normal price | Visits | Yearly value | Plan price |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| HVAC tune-ups | $129 | 2 | $258 | $19/mo ($228/yr) |
| Plumbing inspection + flush | $149 | 1 | $149 | $12/mo ($144/yr) |
| Pest treatments | $119 | 4 | $476 | $35/mo ($420/yr) |

The plan can be slightly *below* the full value of the visits. You make it back through repair work at your normal margin (minus the member discount), steadier scheduling and much better retention.

**Sanity check:** after the member discount, does a typical repair still make a profit? If not, lower the discount, not the plan price.

## Step 3: Make cancellation easy

"Cancel any time, no fee" sounds like it would increase cancellations. In practice it does the opposite: it makes joining feel safe, and customers who feel trapped are the ones who leave angry.

## Step 4: Pitch it at every job (20 seconds)

> "By the way, most of our regular customers are on our Comfort Plan. It's $19 a month and covers your two tune-ups, plus you jump the line if something breaks and get 15% off repairs. Want me to send you the details?"

## Step 5: Show the value to keep members

The biggest reason members renew is seeing what they got. After every included visit, send a short summary by text or email: what you checked, what you found, what you fixed. Once a year, send a recap.

## Billing

Most field-service and invoicing apps can bill recurring plans automatically. Search their help center for "recurring invoices," "service plans" or "memberships."
