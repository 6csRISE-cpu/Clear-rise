# Faceless Short-Video Scripts (12)

**Format:** vertical 9:16, 20–40 seconds. Visual = phone-screen text conversation mockup or bold on-screen captions over simple b-roll (truck, tools, a phone ringing). Voiceover = calm AI voice or text-to-speech. Captions always on.
**End card (every video):** "Free: 5 texts that win back missed calls → link in bio."
**Post:** YouTube Shorts, TikTok, Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels. Same file everywhere.
**Make 12 at once** in CapCut or Canva (templates: "text message story," "iMessage chat").

---

### 1. "The 5-minute text that saves the job"
- **Hook (0–3s):** "You missed a call. Here's the text that gets the job anyway."
- **Body:** show phone: missed call → auto-text appears: "Hi, this is Mike with Ridgeline Heating. Sorry I missed your call, I'm on a job. What can I help with?" → homeowner replies "AC isn't cooling" → booked.
- **VO:** "Most homeowners call three companies. The first one to reply wins. Set this up once and it sends itself."
- **CTA:** end card.

### 2. "Text this, not that: quote follow-up"
- **Hook:** "Stop texting 'just checking in.'"
- **Body:** ❌ "Just checking in on the quote!" → ✅ "If the quote's more than you planned, I can do a smaller version for around $X. Want me to send it?"
- **VO:** "Give them an option, not a nudge. It lets them say yes without admitting the price scared them."

### 3. "The break-up text"
- **Hook:** "The text that revives dead quotes."
- **Body:** show Day-21 text: "I'll close out your quote this week so I'm not bugging you. If you went another way, totally fine. Mind telling me why?" → reply: "Actually can we still do it?"
- **VO:** "Honest, easy to answer, and surprisingly effective."

### 4. "Your best leads are in your invoices"
- **Hook:** "You already have 200 customers who'd book again."
- **Body:** spreadsheet scroll → filter by last job date → rebooking text.
- **VO:** "Past customers trust you. They just forget. Send them a reminder when the job's due."

### 5. "One sentence = more Google reviews"
- **Hook:** "The honest way plumbers get more Google reviews."
- **Body:** caption of the in-person ask ("Would you mind leaving us an honest Google review?") → text with review link.
- **VO:** "Ask every customer, same words, then text the link. Never offer anything for a review, and never ask only the happy ones. Google doesn't allow either."

### 6. "Voicemail greeting fix"
- **Hook:** "Your voicemail greeting is losing you jobs."
- **Body:** bad greeting text vs good one ("You'll get a text from this number in a few minutes, just reply").
- **VO:** "Tell callers to expect your text so they don't ignore it."

### 7. "Cancellation → rebooked in one text"
- **Hook:** "Customer cancels. Don't just say 'ok.'"
- **Body:** "No problem at all! Want me to move you to Thursday, or would you rather pick a time?"
- **VO:** "A cancellation is a postponement, if you rebook within 24 hours."

### 8. "The $19/month idea"
- **Hook:** "How small HVAC shops get paid in January."
- **Body:** maintenance plan graphic: 2 tune-ups, priority service, 15% off repairs, $19/mo.
- **VO:** "Members give you booked work in the slow months, and they don't shop around."

### 9. "Price shopper script"
- **Hook:** "'How much do you charge?' Say this."
- **Body:** "Most jobs like that land between $X and $Y depending on Z. The visit to give you an exact price is free. Thursday or Friday?"
- **VO:** "Give a range, then book. Don't quote blind over the phone."

### 10. "The referral text"
- **Hook:** "They just left you 5 stars. Now send this."
- **Body:** referral text with a two-sided reward.
- **VO:** "Happy customers will refer you. Most just need to be asked."

### 11. "How much are missed calls costing you?"
- **Hook:** "30 calls a week. 20% missed. Do the math."
- **Body:** calculator animation: 26 missed calls/mo × 40% would book × $350 = $3,640/mo at risk.
- **VO:** "Try the free calculator. Link in bio."
- **CTA:** calculator link (homepage #calculator).

### 12. "The reminder pair that kills no-shows"
- **Hook:** "Two texts that stop no-shows."
- **Body:** 48-hour reminder with "Reply C to confirm" + morning-of text.
- **VO:** "Confirmations catch reschedules early, so your day doesn't fall apart."
